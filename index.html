Relay

Your own team of AI assistants. Chat with Relay Bot, and it runs the rest: bots for school, work, and email that keep working while you're away.

Download for Mac Download for Windows

Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) · Windows 10 or 11

Relay is in a private test. To use it you need an invite from Cooper: an account, and access to the Relay server.

What it does

A team, not a chatbot

Relay Bot hands your requests to the right bot and sets up new ones when you need them.

Works while you're away

Bots check your email, follow your routines, and message each other, then tell you what they did.

Remembers you

A 3D map of what your bots know about you, with a profile Relay Bot keeps up to date each morning.

Pages, calendar, apps

Pages the bots keep current, one calendar for you and your bots, and small apps built for you.

Getting started

  1. Ask Cooper for an invite. You'll get a Tailscale invite and your Relay sign-in.
  2. Install Tailscale and accept the invite. It's how Relay reaches the server privately.
  3. Download Relay above and install it.
    Mac: open the file and drag Relay into Applications. The first time, if macOS says it can't check the app, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
    Windows: run the setup. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info › Run anyway.
  4. Open Relay and sign in.
